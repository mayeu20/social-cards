THE PATTERN, paste it above any decision you are about to sign off:

CHALLENGE the decision below. Argue the other side as if you had to defeat it in a review.

Produce:
1. The strongest case against the decision — the best argument for a different choice.
2. The best alternative — the option this decision is dismissing too quickly, and its merits.
3. Hidden assumptions — beliefs the decision rests on that may not hold.
4. What we will regret — the most likely way this looks wrong in six months.
5. What would justify it anyway — the conditions under which the decision is still correct.

Be specific and fair to both sides. Do not agree just to be agreeable. If the decision is sound, say why the opposing case fails.
