DeepSeek's pricing page now runs peak and off-peak rates. Off-peak is half of peak, and peak is only 01:00 to 04:00 and 06:00 to 10:00 UTC. Any batch job moved outside those seven hours costs half as much, for one scheduler change.

Andrew Ng's AI Engineering Skills Map comes from an analysis of over 10,000 job postings plus expert interviews. The four skills: building and deploying AI apps, software engineering fundamentals, using coding agents, shaping the build. Not one of the four is a model name.
