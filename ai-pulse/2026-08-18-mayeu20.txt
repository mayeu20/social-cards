Two unpublished NeurIPS 2026 papers, agents given six days and $3,000 in API credits to answer their core research question. The original authors graded the output and rejected both. Engineering held up, research judgment did not.

Three in today's pulse, two below.

---

Linear's own data: teams with a coding agent connected went from 21 weekly PRs to 65 in two years, while teams without went 8 to 10. Linear counts PRs opened rather than merged, and says the two cohorts were never comparable to start with. Motion measures easily, value does not.

---

A new paper evolves prompts that spread from agent to agent, surviving a context wipe between sessions. Harmful payloads propagate worse than benign ones, frontier models resist better, and a short caution added to the system prompt gave near-total immunity.
