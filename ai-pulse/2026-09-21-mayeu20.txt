Open-weight models ran 56% of Vercel AI Gateway's tokens in August and 14% of its spend, the first month they took most of the volume. Up from 7% in December.

Volume moved. The money did not.

Three for today's AI Pulse. The next two are below.
---
A new paper: k agents in one container sharing a log match the success rate of 4k independent agents on ARC-AGI-3. Four of them squeezed an MNIST classifier to 1,957 bytes at 99.4% accuracy, past the best known human solution.

Parallel tries are not a team.
---
Google open-sourced Agent Substrate, a Kubernetes runtime that resumes a suspended agent in under 500ms and packs over 1,000 dormant agents per host. On GKE it is non-production only, with production GA behind an allowlist.

Idle agents are a billing problem first.
