DeepSeek's dsh-superagent passed 11,000 stars in two days. Its README says much of its own engineering was done by coding agents, and calls it research code, not a product.

Labelling it research code is rarer than it should be.

AI Pulse x3. Two more under this.
---
UNCUT shipped 1 Nov: a coding agent harness in one 7 MB static binary, four tools, no telemetry. It sends the whole conversation every call and exits with an error if the provider truncates, instead of calling a partial answer success.

Failing loudly beats silent context loss.
---
Craft Anything landed 31 Oct and has 2,400 stars: point it at a site, app or game and a coding agent studies the original, builds its own version, then diffs the result against the original. Pure Rust, early alpha.

Clean room is a design claim, not a legal conclusion.
