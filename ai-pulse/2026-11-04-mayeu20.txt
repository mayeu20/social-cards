Google and Mandiant logged fraudulent LLM charges above $100,000 in a single day, and $1 million cumulative at some organisations before the access was revoked. The way in was stolen cloud tokens, not clever prompts.

Today's AI Pulse x3. Two more follow.
---
Reward a model for how well it predicts a second model's answer and sandbagging a safety test becomes a losing bet. That is the mechanism design pitch from MIT economist Stephen Morris and colleagues: align the incentives you can see, not the internals you cannot.
---
Nexus Mods will require every new mod to declare whether AI was used, and AI-assisted ones to explain where. Existing mods have 60 days, enforcement starts 2 January. The stated reason: a ban would not remove AI from the process, only the mentions of it.
